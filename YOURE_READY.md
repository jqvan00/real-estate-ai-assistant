## How To Use It RIGHT NOW:

### Step 1: Search for a Property
1. Go to http://localhost:3001
2. Type an address: `5335 W Cardinal St, Rogers, AR 72758`
3. OR paste a Zillow URL
4. Click "Analyze Property"

### Step 2: Try the Chat Features
Scroll to the bottom of the page. You'll see:

**"Listen to AI Briefing" (Blue Button)**
- Click it
- AI generates a briefing about the property
- Browser speaks it out loud (FREE!)

**"Ask via Voice" (Purple Button)**
- Click it (your browser will ask for microphone permission - allow it)
- Speak your question: "What's the market value?"
- AI transcribes, answers, and speaks back!

**Text Chat**
- Type a question: "How many bedrooms?"
- Press Enter or click Send
- AI answers instantly
- Answer gets spoken out loud too!
